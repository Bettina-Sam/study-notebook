Metals, non-metals, metalloids

Metals

Lustrous, hard, malleable, good conductors. Mercury is the only metal liquid at room temperature.

Non-metals

Generally dull, soft, poor conductors. Bromine is the only non-metal liquid at room temperature.

Metalloids

Show properties of both metals and non-metals — e.g. silicon, arsenic, antimony, boron.

Elements in the body

~99% of body mass is just 6 elements: oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium, phosphorus.
