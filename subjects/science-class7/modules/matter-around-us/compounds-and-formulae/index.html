Read a chemical
formula like a sentence

A compound is a pure substance formed when atoms of two or more elements combine in a fixed proportion. Its chemical formula tells you which elements, and how many atoms of each.

01. Decode a Formula

What does H₂O actually say?

The subscript after each element's symbol tells you how many atoms of it are in one molecule. No subscript means just 1.

Worked Example

Example
Water and its elements hydrogen and oxygen have very different properties. Why?
Concept used: compounds have properties entirely different from their constituent elements.
1
At room temperature, hydrogen and oxygen both exist as gases.
2
Water, their compound, exists as a liquid at room temperature.
3
Key point — oxygen supports fire, but water is used to put fire out. Chemically combining elements creates something with entirely new properties.

Elements vs compounds

Element

The simplest substance — atoms are its fundamental particles.

Compound

Two or more elements chemically combined in fixed proportion — molecules are its fundamental particles.

Separating a compound

Only chemical methods can split a compound into its elements — not physical ones like filtration.

Chemical formula

A symbolic representation of one molecule — tells you the elements present and the number of atoms of each.
