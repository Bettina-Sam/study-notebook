Class 7 Science
Unit 3

Matter Around Us

Everything around you — this page, the air you breathe, your own body — is built from the same tiny building blocks. This unit zooms into atoms and molecules, sorts elements from compounds, and shows what heat actually does to them.

Learn in this order

Start with atoms and molecules — the vocabulary for everything else. Sort elements by their properties, then see how they combine into compounds with a chemical formula. Count atoms for atomicity, then finish with what heat actually does to all of this.

Verified against the 2025 Term-1 textbook

Learning Objectives

  • Know about the molecules of elements and compounds.
  • Write the symbols of common elements.
  • Calculate the atomicity of commonly used elements.
  • Know about the occurrence of elements and compounds in nature and the human body / air.
  • Understand the effects of temperature on solid, liquid and gas.
