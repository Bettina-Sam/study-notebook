Definitions and molecule types

Atom

The basic, smallest unit of matter that exhibits the properties of its element.

Molecule

Two or more atoms chemically combined; exhibits the properties of matter and has individual existence.

Monatomic

One atom only — e.g. inert gases.

Diatomic

Two atoms — e.g. oxygen, nitrogen, hydrogen.

Triatomic

Three atoms — e.g. ozone, carbon dioxide.

Polyatomic

More than three atoms — e.g. phosphorus (P₄), sulphur (S₈).
