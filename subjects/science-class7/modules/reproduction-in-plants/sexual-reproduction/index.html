Seed is produced from a flower through pollination and fertilization. This is sexual reproduction — and it starts with the four whorls of a flower.

01. Parts of a Flower

A complete flower has four whorls, from outside in: calyx (sepals), corolla (petals), androecium (stamens — male), gynoecium (pistil — female).

Pollination, then fertilization

Pollination

Transfer of pollen grains from the anther to the stigma.

Self pollination

Anther to stigma of the same flower or same plant — no new variation.

Cross pollination

Anther of one flower to stigma of another plant — introduces variation; needs pollinators like wind, insects, birds.

Fertilization

Fusion of male and female gametes, forming a zygote inside the ovule.

After fertilization

Ovary → fruit. Ovule → seed. Petals, androecium, style and stigma typically fall off.
