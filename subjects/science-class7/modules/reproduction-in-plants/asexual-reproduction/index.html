The four methods

Vegetative propagation

Root, stem or leaf grows into a new plant — e.g. potato, sugarcane, yam.

Budding

A small protuberance grows and detaches from the parent — e.g. yeast.

Fragmentation

A filament breaks into pieces, each growing into a new individual — e.g. Spirogyra.

Spore formation

Non-flowering plants like algae, fungi, moss and ferns form spores in unfavourable conditions.
