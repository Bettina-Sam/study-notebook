Root, stem and leaf modifications

Root — storage

Carrot (conical), radish (spindle), beet root (top-shaped) — all swollen tap roots.

Root — support

Banyan's prop roots, sugarcane's stilt roots grow from stems for extra support.

Stem — aerial

Opuntia's stem is flattened and green to do photosynthesis; leaves reduce to spines to save water.

Stem — underground

Ginger and turmeric (rhizome), potato (tuber), onion (bulb), Colocasia (corm) — all store food.

Leaf — tendrils and traps

Pea's leaflets become climbing tendrils; Nepenthes' leaf becomes a flask that digests insects for nitrogen.
