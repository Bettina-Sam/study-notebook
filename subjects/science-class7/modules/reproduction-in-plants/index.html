Unit 5

Reproduction and Modification in Plants

A flower isn't just decoration — it's a plant's reproductive machinery. This unit follows a flower into a fruit, explores the plants that skip flowers entirely, and reveals how ordinary roots, stems and leaves quietly transform for entirely different jobs.

Learn in this order

Start with the flower's parts and how pollination leads to fertilization and a fruit. Contrast that with asexual reproduction, which skips seeds entirely. Finish with the surprising ways roots, stems and leaves get reshaped for storage, support, or catching insects.

Verified against the 2025 Term-1 textbook

Learning Objectives

  • Understand how a flower becomes a fruit with seed through pollination and fertilization.
  • Acquire knowledge about pollination and pollinators.
  • Differentiate self pollination and cross pollination in plants.
  • Know about the modification of root, stem and leaves.
  • Understand how these modifications are useful to animals and humans.
