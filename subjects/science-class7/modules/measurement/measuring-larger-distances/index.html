Unit 1 · Measurement 1.5 Measuring Larger Distances

When kilometres
get too small

For distances to stars, astronomers need bigger units than the kilometre. Two of them: the astronomical unit (for the solar system) and the light year (for deep space).

01. Astronomical Unit (AU)

Earth's orbit isn't a circle

Earth orbits the Sun in an ellipse, so the Earth–Sun distance changes every day: about 147.1 million km at perihelion (closest) and 152.1 million km at aphelion (farthest). The average, 149.6 million km, is defined as one astronomical unit (1 AU).

DragMove Earth around its orbit and watch the Earth–Sun distance change.
Perihelion — 147.1 million km
Key idea

1 AU = 149.6 million km = 1.496 × 10¹¹ m. Neptune sits about 30 AU from the Sun — thirty times farther out than Earth.

02. Light Year

How far light travels in a year

Even AU becomes unwieldy for stars — the nearest one, Proxima Centauri, is 2,68,770 AU away. So astronomers use the light year: the distance light travels in vacuum in one year, at 3 × 10⁸ m/s.

DragChange the travel time and watch how far light gets.
Distance = 3 × 10⁸ × 1
= 3 × 10⁸ m (that's most of the way around the Earth, 7.5 times!)

Worked Example

Example
Show that one light year equals about 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m.
Concept used: distance = speed × time, with time converted to seconds in one year.
1
Seconds in one (non-leap) year.
$365 \times 24 \times 60 \times 60 = 3.153 \times 10^7 \text{ s}$
2
Speed of light in vacuum.
$3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$
3
Distance = speed × time.
$3 \times 10^8 \times 3.153 \times 10^7$
4
Final answer
$= 9.46 \times 10^{15} \text{ m}$

Try Yourself

Exercise
Proxima Centauri is 4.22 light-years from Earth. If 1 light year ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m, roughly how many × 10¹⁵ m away is it? (round to 1 decimal place)
Your answer (× 10¹⁵ m):
Multiply 4.22 by 9.46.
Answer: 4.22 × 9.46 ≈ 39.9 × 10¹⁵ m.

Definitions to remember

Astronomical unit

Average distance between Earth and Sun. 1 AU = 149.6 × 10⁶ km = 1.496 × 10¹¹ m.

Light year

Distance travelled by light in vacuum in one year. 1 light year = 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m.

Proxima Centauri

Nearest star to the solar system: 2,68,770 AU, or 4.22 light-years away.

Galactic centre

Earth is located about 25,000 light-years from the centre of our galaxy.
