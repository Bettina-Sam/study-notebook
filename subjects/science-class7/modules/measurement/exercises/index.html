Numerical problems

Numerical 1
A circular disc has a radius of 10 cm. Find the area of the disc in cm² (use π = 3.14).
Your answer (cm²):
Area = π r² = 3.14 × 10 × 10 = 314 cm².
Numerical 2
A school playground measures 800 m × 500 m. Find its area in m².
Your answer (m²):
Area = length × breadth = 800 × 500 = 400,000 m².
Numerical 3
A liquid of mass 250 g fills a space of 1000 cc. Find its density in g/cm³.
Your answer (g/cm³):
Density = Mass / Volume = 250 / 1000 = 0.25 g/cm³.
