Unit 1 · Measurement 1.2 Area

How much space
on a flat surface

Area is a measure of how much space is there on a flat surface — a derived quantity, obtained by multiplying lengths. Its SI unit is m² (square metre).

02. Irregular Shapes

Counting squares on a graph sheet

A leaf has no formula for area. Place its outline on a graph sheet and count: M = whole squares enclosed, N = squares more than half, P = squares exactly half, Q = squares less than half.

Approximate area = M + (3/4)N + (1/2)P + (1/4)Q sq. cm

Worked Example

Example (Problem 1.2)
Find the area of a circle whose radius is 7 m. (Take π = 22/7)
Concept used: Area of circle = π × r².
1
Start with the formula.
Area $= \pi r^2$
2
Substitute r = 7, π = 22/7.
$= \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7$
3
Simplify.
$= 22 \times 7$
4
Final answer
$= 154 \text{ m}^2$
