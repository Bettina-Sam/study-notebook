Reading motion straight
off a graph's slope

A distance-time graph's slope is speed. A speed-time graph's slope is acceleration, and the area under it is the distance covered. Pick a journey and watch both graphs draw themselves.

Key idea

A straight, tilted distance-time line means uniform speed. A curving-upward distance-time line means the object is speeding up.

Reading the two graphs

Distance-time, zero slope

Object at rest.

Distance-time, constant slope

Uniform speed.

Distance-time, increasing slope

Speed is increasing (concave curve).

Speed-time, sloped line

Constant, non-zero acceleration; area under the line gives distance.
