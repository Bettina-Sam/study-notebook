Numerical problems

Numerical 1
Geetha takes 15 minutes (900 s) to cycle to school at a speed of 2 m/s. Find the distance to her school, in metres.
Your answer (m):
Distance = speed × time = 2 × 900 = 1800 m.
Numerical 2
A car starts from rest and reaches a velocity of 20 m/s in 10 s. Find its acceleration in m/s².
Your answer (m/s²):
Acceleration = (v−u)/t = (20−0)/10 = 2 m/s².
