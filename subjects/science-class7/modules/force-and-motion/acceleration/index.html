Types of acceleration

Positive acceleration

Velocity increases with time.

Negative acceleration

Also called deceleration or retardation — velocity decreases with time.

Uniform acceleration

The change in velocity is the same for every unit of time.

Non-uniform acceleration

The change in velocity is different for every unit of time.
