Hygiene and body care essentials

Health

A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being — not merely the absence of disease.

Personal hygiene

Regular bathing, brushing, hand-washing, and clean clothes.

Community hygiene

Clean surroundings, covered drains, proper waste segregation and disposal.

Eye care

80% of what we perceive comes through sight — protect eyes from strain and infection.

Hair care

Regular washing removes excess oil, sweat and dead skin cells that build up on the scalp.
