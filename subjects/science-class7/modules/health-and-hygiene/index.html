Unit 6

Health and Hygiene

Why do some sicknesses need nothing but rest, while others need a doctor right away? This unit builds the daily habits that keep you well, sorts diseases by how they spread, and covers the first-aid steps that matter most in the first few minutes.

Learn in this order

Start with the daily habits of personal and community hygiene, and how to care for teeth, eyes and hair. Learn to tell communicable diseases from non-communicable ones, then finish with practical first aid for burns and cuts.

Verified against the 2025 Term-1 textbook

Learning Objectives

  • Describe ways to take care of the body.
  • Know how to take care of the teeth, eye, hair and follow hygienic habits.
  • Understand communicable and non-communicable diseases.
  • Know common ailments and their remedies.
  • Know first aid and safety measures.
