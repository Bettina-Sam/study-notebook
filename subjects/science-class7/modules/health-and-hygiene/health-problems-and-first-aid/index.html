Anaemia and burn degrees

Anaemia

Caused by low iron intake; signs include pale skin, pale gums, weakness and fatigue. Prevented with iron-rich food.

First-degree burn

Affects only the outer skin layer (epidermis).

Second-degree burn

Damages the epidermis and the layer beneath (dermis).

Third-degree burn

Destroys the skin to full depth and damages underlying tissue — needs urgent medical care.

Self-protection

Wear gloves when helping someone bleeding, to protect against blood-borne infections.
