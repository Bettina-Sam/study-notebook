Communicable vs non-communicable

Communicable

Spread from person to person through air, water, food, or vectors — e.g. TB, cholera, typhoid, hepatitis, chickenpox, rabies.

Non-communicable

Not caused by germs, cannot spread — e.g. asthma, allergies, anaemia, malnutrition.

Vaccines

A biological preparation giving active acquired immunity — e.g. BCG, Polio, MMR, given in early childhood.
