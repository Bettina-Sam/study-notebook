Unit 4

Atomic Structure

If every atom is built from the same three particles, what makes carbon different from iron? This unit goes inside the atom — through a century of competing theories, down to protons, neutrons, electrons, and the numbers that identify every element.

Learn in this order

Trace how the atomic model evolved, meet the three sub-atomic particles, learn how atomic and mass number identify an element, then see how valency decides which atoms bond with which.

Verified against the 2025 Term-1 textbook

Learning Objectives

  • Know the structure of an atom.
  • Know the position of the sub-atomic particles.
  • Understand and compare the properties of sub-atomic particles.
  • Understand the terms atomic number and mass number.
  • Calculate the number of protons, electrons and neutrons in an atom.
  • Understand the term valency.
