The three theories

Dalton (1808)

Matter is made of tiny, indivisible, solid, spherical particles called atoms — but said nothing about charge.

Thomson (1897)

"Watermelon" model — negatively charged electrons embedded in a positively charged sphere; discovered the electron experimentally.

Rutherford (1911)

Gold-foil experiment showed atoms are mostly empty space, with a tiny, dense, positive nucleus; electrons orbit around it.
