Unit 4 · Atomic Structure 4.2 The Sub-Atomic Particles

Three particles,
every atom

Every atom of every element is built from just three particles: protons and neutrons packed into the nucleus, and electrons orbiting around it.

01. Meet the Particles

Charge, mass, and location

TryClick a particle to see its properties.
Proton — charge +1, mass 1.6726 × 10⁻²⁷ kg, found in the nucleus.
Key idea

Protons and neutrons together are called nucleons. An electron's mass is negligible compared to a proton or neutron — so an atom's mass depends almost entirely on its nucleus.

Table 4.1 — Charge and mass of sub-atomic particles

Proton

Charge +1, mass 1.6726 × 10⁻²⁷ kg, discovered by Goldstein.

Electron

Charge −1, mass 9.1093 × 10⁻³¹ kg, discovered by J.J. Thomson.

Neutron

Charge 0, mass 1.6749 × 10⁻²⁷ kg, discovered by James Chadwick.

Electrical neutrality

Total negative charge (electrons) equals total positive charge (protons), so the atom overall is neutral.
