Understand

The Cartesian plane

The horizontal number line is the x-axis, the vertical one is the y-axis, and their intersection is the origin $O(0,0)$. A point $(x,y)$ has abscissa $x$ and ordinate $y$.

Quadrants: I $(+,+)$, II $(-,+)$, III $(-,-)$, IV $(+,-)$.
