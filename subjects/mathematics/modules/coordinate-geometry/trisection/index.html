5.5

Points of Trisection

Move from “halfway” to “one-third” and “two-thirds” along a segment.

HookUnderstandSee ItExploreWorked ExampleYour TurnQuick Check
Understand

Two points divide a segment into three equal parts

If $A(x_1,y_1)$ and $B(x_2,y_2)$ are endpoints, the first trisection point is one-third of the way from A and the second is two-thirds.

$P=\left(\frac{2x_1+x_2}3,\frac{2y_1+y_2}3\right)$    $Q=\left(\frac{x_1+2x_2}3,\frac{y_1+2y_2}3\right)$
