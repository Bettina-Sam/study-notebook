Section Formula

One formula locates any internal division point on a segment.

HookUnderstandSee ItExploreWorked ExampleYour TurnQuick Check
Understand

Divide in any ratio

$P\left(\frac{mx_2+nx_1}{m+n},\frac{my_2+ny_1}{m+n}\right)$ where $AP:PB=m:n$

Midpoint and trisection are special cases of this one idea.
