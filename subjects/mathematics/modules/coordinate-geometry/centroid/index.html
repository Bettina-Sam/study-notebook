The balance point of a triangle

$G\left(\frac{x_1+x_2+x_3}3,\frac{y_1+y_2+y_3}3\right)$

The centroid is where all three medians meet. It divides each median in the ratio $2:1$ from the vertex.

Euler line connection

The textbook notes that orthocentre $H$, centroid $G$ and circumcentre $S$ lie on the Euler line, with $G$ dividing $HS$ in the ratio $2:1$ from the orthocentre. In an equilateral triangle, orthocentre, incentre, centroid and circumcentre coincide.
