Exercise 5.3

Mid-point Formula

Original textbook practice rebuilt as visual learning cards. Attempt first; then interact, check hints, reveal the answer and read the worked steps.

1
Find the midpoint of $(-2,3)$ and $(-6,-5)$.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
Average x-coordinates and y-coordinates separately.
x: $(-2-6)/2$; y: $(3-5)/2$.
Answer: (-4,-1)
1
Midpoint $=(-4,-1)$.
✓
Closure: (-4,-1)
2
Find the midpoint of $(8,-2)$ and $(-8,0)$.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
Average coordinate pairs.
Opposite x-values average to zero.
Answer: (0,-1)
1
Midpoint $=(0,-1)$.
✓
Closure: (0,-1)
3
Find midpoint of $(a,b)$ and $(a+2b,2a-b)$.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
Average each coordinate.
Simplify numerator before dividing by 2.
Answer: $(a+b,a)$
1
$x=(2a+2b)/2=a+b$, $y=(2a)/2=a$.
✓
Closure: $(a+b,a)$
4
The centre of a circle is $(-4,2)$ and one end of a diameter is $(-3,7)$. Find the other end.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
The centre is the midpoint of the diameter endpoints.
Reverse the midpoint equations.
Answer: (-5,-3)
1
If B is $(x,y)$, $(-3+x)/2=-4$ and $(7+y)/2=2$, giving $(-5,-3)$.
✓
Closure: (-5,-3)
5
The midpoint of the sides of a triangle are $(2,4),(-2,3),(5,2)$. Find the vertices.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
Use the relation vertex = sum of adjacent midpoints − opposite midpoint.
Label the three side-midpoints consistently.
Answer: (9,3), (-5,5), (1,1)
1
Applying the midpoint inversion gives the three vertices in the textbook answer.
✓
Closure: (9,3), (-5,5), (1,1)
6
For the right-triangle/hypotenuse exercise, show the midpoint of the hypotenuse is equidistant from all three vertices.
DRAG ENDPOINTS — watch midpoint
Find the hypotenuse midpoint first.
Compute its distance to each vertex.
Answer: The three distances are equal.
1
All three distances match, confirming the standard right-triangle midpoint result.
✓
Closure: The three distances are equal.
