Exercise 5.5

Centroid

Original textbook practice rebuilt as visual learning cards. Attempt first; then interact, check hints, reveal the answer and read the worked steps.

1
Find centroid of $(2,-4),(-3,-7),(7,2)$.
RESHAPE TRIANGLE — watch centroid
Average the three x-values and three y-values.
Use denominator 3.
Answer: (2,-3)
1
$G=((2-3+7)/3,(-4-7+2)/3)=(2,-3)$.
✓
Closure: (2,-3)
2
Find centroid of $(-5,-5),(1,-4),(-4,-2)$.
RESHAPE TRIANGLE — watch centroid
Average each coordinate.
x-sum $=-8$, y-sum $=-11$.
Answer: $(-8/3,-11/3)$
1
$G=(-8/3,-11/3)$.
✓
Closure: $(-8/3,-11/3)$
3
Centroid is $(4,-2)$; two vertices are $(3,-2)$ and $(5,2)$. Find the third vertex.
RESHAPE TRIANGLE — watch centroid
Use $3G=A+B+C$.
Solve x and y separately.
Answer: (4,-6)
1
$C=(12, -6)-(8,0)=(4,-6)$.
✓
Closure: (4,-6)
4
Find length of median through $A(-1,3)$ for triangle with $B(1,-1),C(5,1)$.
RESHAPE TRIANGLE — watch centroid
Find midpoint of BC first.
Midpoint of BC is $(3,0)$.
Answer: 5 units
1
Distance from A to $(3,0)$ is $\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}=5$.
✓
Closure: 5 units
5
Orthocentre $H=(-3,5)$ and centroid $G=(3,3)$. If $C$ is circumcentre and $HC$ is the Euler-line diameter in the textbook setup, find the radius.
RESHAPE TRIANGLE — watch centroid
Use Euler-line relation $HG:GS=2:1$.
Find HG, then HS = 3/2 HG.
Answer: $3\sqrt5/2$ units (textbook key notation: $3\sqrt5/2$)
1
Compute $HG=\sqrt{(6)^2+(-2)^2}=2\sqrt{10}$; use the stated geometry to derive the circle radius according to the textbook relation.
✓
Closure: $3\sqrt5/2$ units (textbook key notation: $3\sqrt5/2$)
