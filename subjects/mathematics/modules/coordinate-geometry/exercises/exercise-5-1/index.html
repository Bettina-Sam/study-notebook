Exercise 5.1

Plotting & Quadrants

Original textbook practice rebuilt as visual learning cards. Attempt first; then interact, check hints, reveal the answer and read the worked steps.

1
Plot $P(-7,6), Q(7,-2), R(-6,-7), S(3,5), T(3,9)$ and identify their quadrants.
PLOT BEFORE ANSWERING
Use signs of x and y before plotting.
I $(+,+)$, II $(-,+)$, III $(-,-)$, IV $(+,-)$.
Answer: P: II; Q: IV; R: III; S: I; T: I

3
Plot the given points with equal y-coordinates and describe the figure formed.
PLOT BEFORE ANSWERING

Only x varies.
Answer: A straight line parallel to the x-axis.

✓
Closure: A straight line parallel to the x-axis.
