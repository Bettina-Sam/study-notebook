The Mid-point of a Line Segment

The midpoint formula is not a rule to memorise blindly — it is coordinate averaging.

HookUnderstandSee ItExploreWorked ExampleYour TurnQuick Check
Understand

Average the coordinates

$M\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}2,\frac{y_1+y_2}2\right)$

The midpoint sits halfway horizontally and halfway vertically.
