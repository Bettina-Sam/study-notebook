An address is a coordinate idea

Quick Check

Order matters

Coordinates are written $(x,y)$: horizontal movement first, vertical movement second. Swapping them usually gives a different point.

Rene Descartes’ coordinate idea combines arithmetic, algebra and geometry by representing locations as points and equations as geometric shapes.
