Q3
Represent the following in scientific notation:
(i) $(300000)^2 \times (20000)^4$
1
$= (3.0\times10^{5})^2 \times (2.0\times10^{4})^4 = 9\times10^{10} \times 16\times10^{16}$
2
$= 144 \times 10^{26}$
= $1.44 \times 10^{28}$
(ii) $(0.000001)^{11} \times (0.005)^3$
1
$= (1.0\times10^{-6})^{11} \times (5.0\times10^{-3})^3 = 1\times10^{-66} \times 125\times10^{-9}$
2
$= 125 \times 10^{-75}$
= $1.25 \times 10^{-73}$
(iii) $\dfrac{(0.00003)^6 \times (0.00005)^4}{(0.009)^3 \times (0.05)^2}$
1
Numerator $= (3\times10^{-5})^6 (5\times10^{-5})^4 = 729\times10^{-30} \times 625\times10^{-20} = 455625\times10^{-50} = 4.55625\times10^{-44}$
2
Denominator $= (9\times10^{-3})^3 (5\times10^{-2})^2 = 729\times10^{-9} \times 25\times10^{-4} = 18225\times10^{-13} = 1.8225\times10^{-9}$
3
Ratio $= \dfrac{4.55625}{1.8225}\times10^{-44+9} = 2.5\times10^{-35}$
= $2.5 \times 10^{-35}$

Q5
Simplify:
(i) $(2.75 \times 10^{7}) + (1.23 \times 10^{8})$
1
$= (2.75\times10^{7}) + (12.3\times10^{7}) = 15.05 \times 10^{7}$
= $1.505 \times 10^{8}$
(ii) $(1.598 \times 10^{17}) \times (4.58 \times 10^{15})$
1
$= (1.598 \times 4.58) \times 10^{17+15} = 7.31884 \times 10^{32}$
= $7.31884 \times 10^{32}$
(iii) $(1.02 \times 10^{10}) \times (1.20 \times 10^{-3})$
1
$= (1.02 \times 1.20) \times 10^{10-3} = 1.224 \times 10^{7}$
= $1.224 \times 10^{7}$
(iv) $(8.41 \times 10^{4}) \div (4.3 \times 10^{5})$
1
$= \dfrac{8.41}{4.3} \times 10^{4-5} \approx 1.9558 \times 10^{-1}$
≈ $1.9558 \times 10^{-1}$
