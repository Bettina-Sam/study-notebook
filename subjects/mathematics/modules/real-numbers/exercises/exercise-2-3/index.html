Q3Find any two rational numbers between $2.2360679\ldots$ and $2.236505500\ldots$
Both of these happen to be irrational (close to $\sqrt5$), but rational numbers can still be squeezed in between them.
Any terminating decimal strictly between $2.2360679$ and $2.2365055$ is rational and works — e.g. something starting $2.2361$ or $2.2364$.
