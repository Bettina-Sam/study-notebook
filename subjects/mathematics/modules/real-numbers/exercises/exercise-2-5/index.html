Q1Write the following in the form of $5^n$:
(i) $625$   (ii) $\dfrac15$   (iii) $\sqrt5$   (iv) $\sqrt[3]{125}$
VERIFY: parts (iii)/(iv) in the scanned original lost their radical symbols; √5 and ∛125 are the standard reconstruction for a "write as 5ⁿ" drill.
(i) $625=5^{\ }$
(ii) $\dfrac15=5^{\ }$
(iii) $\sqrt5=5^{\ }$
(iv) $\sqrt[3]{125}=5^{\ }$
Write each number as a power of 5 first (625 = 5⁴), then a root √5 = 5^(1/2), and a root like ∛125 = 125^(1/3) = (5³)^(1/3) = 5¹.
(i) $625=5^4$ (ii) $\dfrac15=5^{-1}$ (iii) $\sqrt5=5^{1/2}$ (iv) $\sqrt[3]{125}=5^1$

Q2Write the following in the form of $4^n$:
(i) $16$   (ii) $8$   (iii) $32$
(i) $16=4^{\ }$
(ii) $8=4^{\ }$
(iii) $32=4^{\ }$
Every one of these is a power of 2 — rewrite it as a power of 2 first, then convert that exponent to an equivalent power of 4 = 2².
(i) $16=4^2$ (ii) $8=2^3=(4^{1/2})^3=4^{3/2}$ (iii) $32=2^5=(4^{1/2})^5=4^{5/2}$
