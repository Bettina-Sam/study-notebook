Angles & Congruence

In the textbook figure, $AB\parallel CD$. Find the marked angle $x$ in part (i).

2
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1:2:3$. Find all three angles.
MOVE THE RAY — watch angle relationships
A triangle has angle sum $180°$.
Let the angles be $x,2x,3x$.
Answer: 30°, 60°, 90°
1
Set $x+2x+3x=180°$, so $6x=180°$ and $x=30°$. Hence the angles are $30°,60°,90°$.
✓
Closure: 30°, 60°, 90°

4
Given $AB=DF$, $\angle ACB=70°$, $\angle ABC=60°$, $\angle DEF=70°$ and $\angle EDF=60°$, prove $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DFE$.
MOVE THE RAY — watch angle relationships
First find the remaining angle of each triangle.
Use the equal side with the matching angles.
Answer: The triangles are congruent by AAS/ASA after matching the third angle.
1
Each triangle has the same two angles; the third angles are also equal by angle sum. Together with $AB=DF$, corresponding data establish congruence.
✓
Closure: The triangles are congruent by AAS/ASA after matching the third angle.
5
In the textbook triangle, the marked angles are $(x+35)°$, $(2x-5)°$ and exterior angle $(4x-15)°$. Find all three interior angles.
MOVE THE RAY — watch angle relationships
Use the exterior-angle theorem.
The exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.
Answer: 80°, 85°, 15°
1
$(4x-15)=(x+35)+(2x-5)$ gives $x=45$. Then the two remote angles are $80°$ and $85°$, and the third interior angle is $15°$.
✓
Closure: 80°, 85°, 15°
