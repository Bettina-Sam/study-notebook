Exercise 4.2

Quadrilaterals

Original textbook practice rebuilt as visual learning cards. Attempt first; then interact, check hints, reveal the answer and read the worked steps.

1
The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio $2:4:5:7$. Find all four angles.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
The interior angles of a quadrilateral sum to $360°$.
Let the common part be $x$.
Answer: 40°, 80°, 100°, 140°
1
$(2+4+5+7)x=360°$, so $18x=360°$ and $x=20°$.
✓
Closure: 40°, 80°, 100°, 140°
2
In quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\angle A=72°$ and $\angle C$ is supplementary to $\angle A$. The other angles are $2x-10$ and $x+4$. Find $x$ and all angles.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
First find $\angle C=180°-72°$.
Use the $360°$ quadrilateral angle sum.
Answer: $x=62$; angles $72°,114°,108°,66°$ (ordered according to the given expressions).
1
Substitute $A=72°$, $C=108°$, and the two algebraic angles into the $360°$ sum; solve $x=62$ and evaluate the expressions.
✓
Closure: $x=62$; angles $72°,114°,108°,66°$ (ordered according to the given expressions).
3
Rectangle $ABCD$ has diagonals intersecting at $O$. If $\angle OAB=46°$, find $\angle OBC$.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
The diagonals of a rectangle are equal and bisect each other.
Use the isosceles triangle formed by half-diagonals.
Answer: 44°
1
In the relevant triangle, equal half-diagonals give equal base angles; combine with the right angle at the rectangle corner to obtain $44°$.
✓
Closure: 44°
4
The diagonals of a rhombus are 12 cm and 16 cm. Find its side.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
Rhombus diagonals bisect each other at right angles.
Half-diagonals are 6 cm and 8 cm.
Answer: 10 cm
1
A side is the hypotenuse of a right triangle: $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$ cm.
✓
Closure: 10 cm
5
Show that the bisectors of the angles of a parallelogram form a rectangle.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
Adjacent angles of a parallelogram are supplementary.
Half of supplementary angles add to $90°$.
Answer: The four angle-bisector intersections form a rectangle.
1
Each adjacent pair of angle bisectors meets at a right angle, so the quadrilateral formed has four right angles and is a rectangle.
✓
Closure: The four angle-bisector intersections form a rectangle.
6
If a triangle and a parallelogram lie on the same base and between the same parallels, prove the area of the triangle is half the area of the parallelogram.
TRACE SIDES & DIAGONALS
Both shapes share the same base and height.
Compare $bh$ with $\tfrac12 bh$.
Answer: $[\triangle]=\tfrac12[\text{parallelogram}]$.
1
Parallelogram area is $bh$; triangle area on the same base and between the same parallels is $\tfrac12 bh$.
✓
Closure: $[\triangle]=\tfrac12[\text{parallelogram}]$.

11
