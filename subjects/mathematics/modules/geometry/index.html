Class 9 Mathematics
Chapter 4

Geometry

You already know angles and shapes by eye — geometry just makes that precise. Start from angle facts you can already guess, build up through quadrilaterals and circles, and finish with the compass-and-straightedge constructions that use everything above.

Learn in this order

Each module leans on the one before it — angle facts first, then the quadrilaterals built from them, then circles, the chords inside them, quadrilaterals inscribed in them, finishing with the constructions (centroid, orthocentre, circumcentre, incentre) that put every idea above to use.

Verified against the 2025 textbook

Learning Outcomes

  • To understand theorems on linear pairs and vertically opposite angles.
  • To understand the angle sum property of triangle.
  • To understand the properties of quadrilaterals and use them in problem solving.
  • To understand, interpret and apply theorems on the chords and the angles subtended by arcs of a circle.
  • To understand, interpret and apply theorems on cyclic quadrilaterals.
  • To construct and locate centroid, orthocentre, circumcentre and incentre of a triangle.
