Cyclic Quadrilaterals

Put four vertices on a circle and angle relationships suddenly become predictable.

HookUnderstandSee ItExploreWorked ExampleQuick Check
Hook

Four points on one circle create a powerful angle rule

A quadrilateral whose four vertices lie on a circle is cyclic. Its opposite angles are supplementary.

$\\angle A+\\angle C=180°$ and $\\angle B+\\angle D=180°$

$C=$ 110°
Understand

Angles from arcs

The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice the angle subtended at any point on the remaining circumference. Angles in the same segment are equal. An angle in a semicircle is $90°$.

If one side of a cyclic quadrilateral is produced, the exterior angle equals the interior opposite angle.
Quick Check

If one cyclic angle is $75°$…

The opposite angle must be $180°-75°=105°$.
