Where do the centres lie?

For an acute triangle the orthocentre lies inside; for an obtuse triangle it lies outside; for a right triangle it is the right-angle vertex. The incentre always lies inside. The circumcentre can be inside, outside, or at the midpoint of the hypotenuse for a right triangle.

Equilateral special case: centroid, orthocentre, circumcentre and incentre all coincide.
