01. Classify by Measure

Rotate the ray

An angle is formed by two rays sharing an end point. As the measure $\theta$ grows from $0°$ to $360°$, the same pair of rays passes through every named type of angle.

0–90°Acute angle
90°Right angle
90–180°Obtuse angle
180°Straight angle
180–360°Reflex angle

02. Complementary & Supplementary

Two angles that add to something fixed

Two angles are complementary if they add to $90°$, and supplementary if they add to $180°$. Neither angle needs to touch the other — it's purely about the sum.

Complement = 50°
Supplement = 140°

03. Linear Pair & Vertically Opposite

Two lines crossing at a point

When a ray stands on a straight line, the two adjacent angles it makes form a linear pair — they always sum to $180°$. When two full lines cross, the angles opposite each other are called vertically opposite angles, and they are always equal.

•
∠AOC = ∠BOD = 50° (vertically opposite)
•
∠COB = ∠DOA = 130° (vertically opposite)
•
∠AOC + ∠COB = 180° (linear pair)

Eight angles, two values

05. Triangle Angle Sum

The angles of any triangle add to 180°

Set two angles of a triangle — the third is never free, it's forced to make the total $180°$. Stretch a side outward and the exterior angle formed equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.

A + B + C = 60° + 70° + 50° = 180°
Exterior angle at B = 130° = A + C (60° + 70°)

Worked Example

Example
The angles of a triangle are in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. Find the measure of each angle.
Concept used: the triangle angle-sum property, $A + B + C = 180°$.
1
Let the angles be $x, 2x, 3x$.
2
Their sum is $180°$.
$x + 2x + 3x = 180°$
3
Simplify and solve for $x$.
$6x = 180° \Rightarrow x = 30°$
4
Final answer
Angles are $30°, 60°, 90°$

Try Yourself

Exercise
Two parallel lines $l$ and $m$ are cut by a transversal. The transversal makes a $65°$ angle with $l$. Find the co-interior angle $x$ it makes with $m$.
Co-interior (allied) angles lie between the parallel lines, on the same side of the transversal.
Co-interior angles are supplementary — they add to $180°$, not equal to each other.
1
Co-interior angles sum to $180°$.
$65° + x = 180°$
2
Solve for $x$.
$x = 115°$
Answer: $x = 115°$.
