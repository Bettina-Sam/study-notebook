Properties of Chords

Move a chord, split it with a perpendicular, and turn circle theorems into right-triangle reasoning.

HookUnderstandSee ItExploreWorked ExampleQuick Check
Understand

The centre sees every chord symmetrically

The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord. This creates two congruent right triangles and powers most chord-length problems.

Explore

Move a chord away from the centre

DRAG DISTANCE — WATCH CHORD LENGTH SHRINK

For radius $r$ and centre-to-chord distance $d$, half the chord is $\\sqrt{r^2-d^2}$.

See It

Equal chords ↔ equal central angles

Equal chords subtend equal angles at the centre; equal chords are also equidistant from the centre.
Quick Check

52 cm diameter, 20 cm chord

Radius is 26 cm and half-chord is 10 cm. The centre-to-chord distance is $\\sqrt{26^2-10^2}=24$ cm.
