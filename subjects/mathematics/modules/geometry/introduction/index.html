Hook

What can a shape tell you?

Geometry is the study of shapes through drawing, comparing and measuring. The chapter begins by turning familiar angle facts into tools, then uses them to reason about quadrilaterals and circles.

Learning outcomes from the textbook: understand linear pairs and vertically opposite angles, triangle angle sum, quadrilateral properties, circle chord and arc theorems, cyclic quadrilaterals, and construct the four centres of a triangle.

Geometry is connected

Angle rules explain triangles. Triangles help prove quadrilateral properties. Chords create triangles inside circles. Cyclic quadrilaterals turn those angle facts into powerful circle results. Practical geometry then uses perpendicular bisectors, medians and angle bisectors to locate special points.

Predict: which triangle centre do you think will always lie inside a triangle? You will test this later with a construction.
