Example
Classify $5t^3 + 6t + 8t^2$ by number of terms, and state its degree and type.
Concept used: count the terms, then compare the highest exponent to the standard names.
1
Write in standard form (highest power first).
$5t^3 + 8t^2 + 6t$
2
Count the terms: $5t^3$, $8t^2$, $6t$.
3 terms → Trinomial
3
Highest exponent is 3.
Degree = 3 → Cubic polynomial
4
Final answer
Cubic trinomial, degree 3

Try Yourself

Exercise
What is the degree of $y^2 - 8$, and what type of polynomial is it (by number of terms)?
The degree is the highest power of $y$ that appears.
Count how many separate terms are added or subtracted: $y^2$ and $-8$.
1
Terms: $y^2$ and $-8$ → 2 terms → Binomial
2
Highest power of $y$ is 2 → degree 2 → Quadratic
Answer: degree 2 (quadratic), a binomial.
