Worked Example

Example
Factorise $x^2 + 10x + 24$.
Concept used: find two numbers that multiply to 24 and add to 10.
1
Need two numbers: product 24, sum 10 → 4 and 6.
2
Split the middle term.
$x^2 + 4x + 6x + 24$
3
Group and factor each pair.
$x(x + 4) + 6(x + 4)$
4
Final answer
$(x + 4)(x + 6)$

Try Yourself

Exercise
Factorise $p^2 - 6p - 16$.
Two numbers: product −16, sum −6 →
One number should be negative and larger in size than the other, since the product is negative.
Try factor pairs of 16: 1×16, 2×8, 4×4 — which pair, with one negative, adds to −6?
1
Numbers: $2$ and $-8$ (product $-16$, sum $-6$)
2
$p^2 + 2p - 8p - 16$
3
$p(p+2) - 8(p+2)$
Answer: $(p+2)(p-8)$
