Chapter 3

Algebra

A polynomial is just a number written in a more flexible language. Once you can add, subtract, multiply and divide them — and read what makes them zero — a huge amount of Class 9 mathematics opens up. Eight modules, building in order from a single letter standing in for a number, up to a full pair of equations you can solve two different ways.

Learn in this order

Each module leans on the one before it — introduce a variable, then a whole polynomial, then what happens when you divide one, then the shortcuts (identities, factorisation, GCD) that make those divisions fast, finishing with two-variable equations, which use everything above.
