Q1350 students each come by bus, bicycle, or on foot: 25 bus, 20 bicycle, 30 foot, 10 by all three. How many use exactly two modes of transport?

First find the sum of all three pairwise overlaps using the union formula. Then remember: (sum of pairwise overlaps) = (exactly two) + 3×(all three), since the all-three group gets counted in every pairwise overlap.
$50=25+20+30-(\text{pairwise sum})+10 \Rightarrow \text{pairwise sum}=35$.
