Q1Which of the following are sets?
(i) The collection of prime numbers up to 100.
(ii) The collection of rich people in India.
(iii) The collection of all rivers in India.
(iv) The collection of good Hockey players.

Grab each numbered token below and fling it into the zone where it belongs.

(i) Yes — a set: for any number you can check for certain whether it's prime. (ii) No — "rich" is not well-defined, people would disagree. (iii) Yes — a set: a river is either officially in India or not. (iv) No — "good" is subjective.
