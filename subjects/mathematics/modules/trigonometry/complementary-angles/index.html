Key relationship
\\sin(90^\\circ-\\theta)=\\cos\\theta,\\quad \\tan(90^\\circ-\\theta)=\\cot\\theta
Worked example

If $A+B=90^\circ$, the side opposite A is adjacent to B. That geometric swap creates the complementary identities.
