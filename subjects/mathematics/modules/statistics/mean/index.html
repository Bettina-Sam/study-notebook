Key relationship
\\bar{x}=\\frac{\\sum x}{n},\\quad \\bar{x}=\\frac{\\sum fx}{\\sum f}
Worked example

For frequency data, each value contributes as many times as its frequency; hence $\sum fx/\sum f$.
